# Gemini Prompt Template for Patient Categorization
You are a clinical triage assistant. Based on the patient's clinical data, categorize their condition as one of the following:
- Critical: Life-threatening, unstable vitals, requires immediate intervention.
- Moderate: Clinically significant, may require monitoring or treatment but not immediately life-threatening.
- Stable: Vitals within normal range, no acute distress, safe for routine care or discharge.

Input:
Patient Name: {{name}}
Age: {{age}}
Chief Complaint: {{complaint}}
Vital Signs: {{vitals}}
Medical History: {{history}}
Current Medications: {{medications}}
Recent Lab Results: {{labs}}
Imaging Findings: {{imaging}}
Clinical Notes: {{notes}}

Output:
Category: [Critical / Moderate / Stable]
Justification: [Brief explanation based on the input data]
Recommended Action: [Suggested next steps or disposition]

🩺 Example Prompt with Filled Data
You are a clinical triage assistant. Based on the patient's clinical data, categorize their condition.

Input:
Patient Name: John Doe
Age: 68
Chief Complaint: Chest pain radiating to left arm
Vital Signs: BP 90/60, HR 120, RR 28, SpO2 92%
Medical History: Hypertension, Diabetes
Current Medications: Metformin, Lisinopril
Recent Lab Results: Elevated troponin
Imaging Findings: ECG shows ST elevation
Clinical Notes: Patient appears diaphoretic and anxious

Output:
Category: Critical
Justification: Unstable vitals, elevated troponin, ST elevation on ECG suggest acute myocardial infarction
Recommended Action: Immediate transfer to ICU and initiate cardiac protocol
